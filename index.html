How long does divorce take?

Every state has its own mandatory waiting period before a divorce can be finalized. Once you have a court date or finalization date from your attorney, you can use Finally Free to count down to that exact moment.

California — 6 months minimum
Texas — 60 days minimum
New York — No mandatory wait
Florida — 20 days minimum
Illinois — No mandatory wait
Arizona — 60 days minimum
Colorado — 91 days minimum
Georgia — 30 days minimum

These are minimums — contested divorces, court backlogs, or complex asset situations can extend timelines significantly. Always rely on dates from your attorney or court order, not general estimates.

Why use a divorce countdown?

Going through a divorce is one of the most emotionally taxing experiences a person can face. Having a concrete date — and watching it approach — can provide something powerful: a tangible sense of forward motion. Finally Free gives you something to look at when the process feels endless.
